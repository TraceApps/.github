<p align="center"><b>Free, self-hosted apps for tracking your life on your own server.</b><br/>
No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud sync unless you opt in.</p>

| App | What it tracks | |
|---|---|---|
| **[NutriTrace](https://github.com/TraceApps/nutritrace)** | Nutrition: foods, meals, macros, and wellness | [Docs](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/nutritrace/) |
| **[LiftTrace](https://github.com/TraceApps/lifttrace)** | Weightlifting: every rep, set, and PR | [Docs](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/lifttrace/) |
| **[CookTrace](https://github.com/TraceApps/cooktrace)** | Recipes, pantry, and cooking | [Docs](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/cooktrace/) |
| **[NoteTrace](https://github.com/TraceApps/notetrace)** | Notes that are quick to capture and work offline | [Docs](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/notetrace/) |

Each app runs as a single Docker container, with a web app, an Android app, and Wear OS where it helps. They work on their own, and NutriTrace connects with LiftTrace and CookTrace when you run them together. Not sure where to start? [Choose your app](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/getting-started/choose/).

From the same developer: **[Fathom](https://github.com/Fathom-Media/fathom)**, an all-in-one Jellyfin client for desktop and Android.

### Principles

- **Your data stays on your hardware.** No telemetry, no third-party accounts.
- **Free, forever.** Everything is AGPL-3.0. No paid tier, and nothing is locked behind a donation.

### Support

The Trace apps are built and maintained by one person. Starring the repos, reporting bugs with detail, and [translating](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/contribute/translations/) all help, and they're free. If you'd like to chip in, support on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/traceapps) goes toward a Mac and an iPhone so the apps can come to Apple devices, and [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/TraceApps?metadata_from=profile) helps with the monthly costs. The details are on the [Support page](https://traceapps.github.io/docs/support/).
